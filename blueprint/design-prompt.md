# Dzuels Educational Foundation — Stitch UI Design Prompt

Use this document as the prompt input for Stitch (or Figma AI, Midjourney, v0, etc.) to generate sample visual mocks for the Dzuels Educational Foundation website rebuild.

---

## 1. Design Brief & Brand Identity

- **Organization:** Dzuels Educational Foundation (https://dzuelsfoundation.org), a Nigerian education and youth empowerment NGO founded in 2015.
- **Core Focus:** Free digital literacy infrastructure, school support, leadership mentoring, and community welfare in Ijero-Ekiti and Southwestern Nigeria.
- **Tone & Aesthetic:** Dignified, warm, human, trustworthy, and modern. Emphasizes genuine photography of Nigerian learners, educators, and community members over stock assets.
- **Color Identity:**
  - **Dark Maroon (Headings & Hero Banners):** `#6A0406`
  - **Crimson Red (Primary CTAs & Links):** `#B4070A`
  - **Warm Cream (Badges, Outlines, Script Accents):** `#F9DEC9`
  - **Soft Mint (Micro-accents, Badges & Checkmarks):** `#56E39F`
  - **Page Background (Light Mode default):** `#FFFFFF`
  - **Section Alternating Background:** `#FFF5ED`
  - **Footer Background:** `#2B0203`
  - **Text Colors:** Body `#3B4456`, Strong `#242F40`, Muted `#6B7385`
- **Typography Pairing:**
  - **Primary Sans:** **Poppins** (400 regular, 500 medium, 600 semi-bold, 700 bold).
  - **Handwritten Accent Script:** **Great Vibes** (400 regular) — applied to exactly *one* spotlight word in select headlines (e.g., "Addressing poverty through quality *education* and skills acquisition").
- **Shapes & Radii:**
  - Cards: `18px` rounded corners with subtle maroon elevation (`0 18px 50px rgba(106,4,6,0.08)`).
  - Buttons & Chips: Full pills (`999px`).
  - Inputs: `12px` rounded with maroon focus ring.

## 2. Stitch Screen Prompt: Homepage (Desktop & Mobile)

5. Who We Are Section: Story overview with a photo of foundation mentors framed by a soft cream offset outline, and a clean link to About Us.

7. DRNICER Values Band: Full-width dark maroon banner showcasing the 7 core foundation values:

11. Upcoming Event Callout: Highlight card for "Ijero Tech Day & 10th Anniversary Celebration" with event date, venue badge, and "Register to Attend" button.

13. Final CTA Banner: Rich maroon gradient container with cream decorative circles, compelling copy: "You too can get involved. Volunteer, donate or partner with us to join our mission.", featuring dual buttons: "Get Involved" and "Donate".
14. Footer: Dark maroon 4-column footer with logo badge, quick links, newsletter signup input with pill submit button, copyright notice and privacy policy link.
